# Creator Discovery Platform

An AI-powered platform that helps brands find creators who fit their campaign goals. Brands describe what they’re looking for, then discover and compare micro-creators using practical filters and clear match scores.

## What it does

- **Find relevant creators:** Search by location, niche, audience size, platform, and engagement.
- **Describe a campaign:** Enter campaign goals, target audience, preferred creator traits, and other requirements.
- **Review AI recommendations:** Explore creators recommended for the campaign, with a match score and the factors that contributed to it.
- **Compare creator profiles:** Review audience and engagement information side by side to help build a shortlist.
- **Support both sides of a collaboration:** Give brands a campaign discovery experience and creators a way to share their profile, location, niche, platforms, and audience details.

## How matching works

The platform uses campaign requirements and creator profile data to identify suitable candidates. Recommendations can consider factors such as niche relevance, location, audience fit, follower range, and engagement. Match scores help brands compare recommendations; they are intended as decision support, not a guarantee of campaign performance.

## Project goal

Make creator discovery easier to navigate and more transparent for brands, while helping creators present the information brands need to evaluate potential partnerships.
